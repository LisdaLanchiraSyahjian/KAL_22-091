#Perkalian Silang (Cross Product)

Cross product adalah operasi antara dua vektor dalam ruang tiga dimensi yang menghasilkan vektor baru yang tegak lurus terhadap kedua vektor awal.

Sifat Sifat perkalian silang :

Rumus :

\[\begin{split} \vec{A} \times \vec{B} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ A_x & A_y & A_z \\ B_x & B_y & B_z \\ \end{vmatrix} = (A_y B_z - A_z B_y)\hat{i} - (A_x B_z - A_z B_x)\hat{j} + (A_x B_y - A_y B_x)\hat{k} \end{split}\]

###Contoh Soal $\( \vec{A} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ -1 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \vec{B} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 4 \\ -2 \end{bmatrix} \)$

###Tentukan hasil dari $\( ( \vec{A} \times \vec{B} ): \)$

\[\begin{split} \vec{A} \times \vec{B} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 1 & 4 & -2 \\ \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[ = \hat{i}((-1)(-2) - (3)(4)) - \hat{j}((2)(-2) - (3)(1)) + \hat{k}((2)(4) - (-1)(1)) \]
\[ = \hat{i}(2 - 12) - \hat{j}(-4 - 3) + \hat{k}(8 + 1) \]
\[ = -10\hat{i} + 7\hat{j} + 9\hat{k} \]
\[\begin{split} \Rightarrow \vec{A} \times \vec{B} = \begin{bmatrix} -10 \\ 7 \\ 9 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

buat contoh soal c(u x v) = (cu) x v = u x (cu) c adalah konstanta

\[\begin{split} \vec{u} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ -1 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \vec{v} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 4 \\ -2 \end{bmatrix}, \quad c = 3 \end{split}\]

Tentukan hasil dari

\[\begin{split} \vec{u} \times \vec{v} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 1 & 4 & -2 \\ \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[ = \hat{i}((-1)(-2) - (3)(4)) - \hat{j}((2)(-2) - (3)(1)) + \hat{k}((2)(4) - (-1)(1)) \]
\[ = \hat{i}(2 - 12) - \hat{j}(-4 - 3) + \hat{k}(8 + 1) \]
\[ = -10\hat{i} + 7\hat{j} + 9\hat{k} \]
\[\begin{split} \Rightarrow \vec{u} \times \vec{v} = \begin{bmatrix} -10 \\ 7 \\ 9 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Sekarang, hitung

\[\begin{split} c(\vec{u} \times \vec{v}) = 3 \begin{bmatrix} -10 \\ 7 \\ 9 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -30 \\ 21 \\ 27 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung :

\[\begin{split} c\vec{u} = 3 \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ -1 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 6 \\ -3 \\ 9 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} (c\vec{u}) \times \vec{v} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 6 & -3 & 9 \\ 1 & 4 & -2 \\ \end{vmatrix} = \hat{i}((-3)(-2) - (9)(4)) - \hat{j}((6)(-2) - (9)(1)) + \hat{k}((6)(4) - (-3)(1)) \end{split}\]
\[ = \hat{i}(6 - 36) - \hat{j}(-12 - 9) + \hat{k}(24 + 3) \]
\[ = -30\hat{i} + 21\hat{j} + 27\hat{k} \]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} -30 \\ 21 \\ 27 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung :

\[\begin{split} c\vec{v} = 3 \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 4 \\ -2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 12 \\ -6 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} \vec{u} \times (c\vec{v}) = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 3 & 12 & -6 \\ \end{vmatrix} = \hat{i}((-1)(-6) - (3)(12)) - \hat{j}((2)(-6) - (3)(3)) + \hat{k}((2)(12) - (-1)(3)) \end{split}\]
\[ = \hat{i}(6 - 36) - \hat{j}(-12 - 9) + \hat{k}(24 + 3) \]
\[ = -30\hat{i} + 21\hat{j} + 27\hat{k} \]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} -30 \\ 21 \\ 27 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} \boxed{ c(\vec{u} \times \vec{v}) = (c\vec{u}) \times \vec{v} = \vec{u} \times (c\vec{v}) = \begin{bmatrix} -30 \\ 21 \\ 27 \end{bmatrix} } \end{split}\]
